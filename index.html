PROJECT 02 ◎ AI / EMBEDDED

AI-Based Fire
Detection System

A computer-vision prototype that detects fire and smoke from a laptop camera feed and uses an ESP32 DevKit V1 to trigger local alerts.

  • Used a laptop camera as the live video source.
  • Built the detection workflow with Python, OpenCV and a trained YOLO model.
  • Sent detection signals over serial communication to an ESP32 DevKit V1.
  • Used the ESP32 to control LED and buzzer alerts.
